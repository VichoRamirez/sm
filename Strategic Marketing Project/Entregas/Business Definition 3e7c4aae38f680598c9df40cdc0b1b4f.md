# Business Definition

Status: Done

The Buisness is defined as a mobile phone and web app, that helps users form end to end on the buy-selling car business, with an ai asistant that, through photos or videos and extra information, scans your car, and helps you with a diagnosis of the car, tells you which parts *might* need be replaced and an estimation of the cost of repairs. With that, its easier to set a fair price.

It also does a market scrape to define the market value of a car according to it’s model, year, mileage, etc.
