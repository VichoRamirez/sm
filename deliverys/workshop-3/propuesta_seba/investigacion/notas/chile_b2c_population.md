# Chile B2C population of used-car buyers and survey conditions (InfoCar research design check, as of 2 Nov 2026)

Scope: official and first-tier Chilean sources used to check the InfoCar B2C draft (persons 18+ in RM, Valparaíso and Biobío who bought a used light or medium vehicle in the last 12 months; about 634,000 purchases a year; cells = seller type × 4 age bands; CAWI plus intercepts at notarías and PRT; legal claims about Ley 21.398 and Ley 21.719).

Summary of corrections to the draft (details and sources below):
1. **Wrong legal claim.** Buying a used car from a company does NOT automatically carry the 6-month legal warranty. Under Ley 19.496 art. 14 and SERNAC's own guidance, the legal warranty applies only to new products when the seller has told the buyer the car is used. A company is bound only by a voluntary warranty it chooses to give, plus its duty to inform. A private one-off seller is not a "proveedor", so consumer law does not apply to that sale at all.
2. **Ley 21.719 date is uncertain.** The law was published on 13 Dec 2024 and is set to take effect on 1 Dec 2026. However, on 28 Oct 2026 the Senate approved a bill (Boletín 18.623-07) that moves this to 15 Oct 2027. As of 30 Oct 2026 the bill was in the Cámara (second step). The 1 Dec 2026 date holds unless the change is published before then.
3. **Population base overstated by about 7%.** CAVEM's "livianos y medianos usados" total includes Comerciales (vans and light commercials), about 7% of used transfers. The draft only covers cars, SUVs and pickups. The base should also be the last 12 months, not calendar 2025.
4. **Seller-type cells have no official benchmark.** No official Chilean statistic splits used purchases by seller type (private vs company). The only figure is an unverified industry estimate (about 60% between private persons).
5. **Two inputs not verified.** The 70.6% three-region share and the CAVEM Anuario 2025 age-band shares could not be checked against the Anuario itself. A CAVEM monthly report gives a consistent three-region share of about 69.9% (July 2025).
6. **Chinese-brand figure does not match.** The share is 12.83% (about 83,000 transfers "en el año", monthly peak 13.77%) per CAVEM as reported on 5–6 Oct 2026. 12.95% was not found anywhere.

## 1. Official statistics on used transfers (CAVEM / Registro Civil): totals, region, buyer type, age, body type, seller type

### Takeaway
CAVEM monthly reports are built on Registro Civil (SRCeI) transfer data. They give totals, region, body type and, in press releases, buyer type (persona natural vs empresa, and men vs women). They do not give seller type. The draft's 84.7% persons share is confirmed (September 2026, one month only). The 3-region share of about 70% is consistent with CAVEM's regional table (69.9% in July 2025). The ~634k base should be cut to about 0.59–0.61 million once commercial vehicles are removed and a trailing-12-month base is used.

### Inferences
- **Recomputed population base (trailing 12 months to Sep 2026, own calculation):**
  - Jan–Sep 2025 = 821,781 − 32,745 = 789,036.
  - So Oct–Dec 2025 = 1,060,919 − 789,036 = 271,883.
  - Trailing 12 months = 821,781 + 271,883 ≈ **1,093,664 transfers**.
  - × 0.706 (3-region share, draft value, unverified) × 0.847 (persons) ≈ **654,000**.
  - × 0.929 to drop Comerciales (≈7.1% in Sep 2026, 6.8% in Jul 2025) ≈ **607,600**.
  - Using the July 2025 regional share (0.699) instead gives ≈ 601,600.
  - Recommended wording: "≈ 0.60 million purchases of used cars/SUVs/pickups by natural persons per year in the 3 regions (range 0.59–0.65 million depending on assumptions)".
- **Transfers are not distinct buyers or household-use purchases.** Persons can buy more than one vehicle. Informal resellers who register cars in their own name (habitual "comerciantes" acting as persona natural) are counted as persons. Some pickups bought by persons are for work use. The figure is therefore an upper bound for the target population of household-use buyers.
- **CAVEM region coding is not documented.** Most likely it reflects the buyer's or registration address in the RVM, but this was not documented in the sources read. Treat region as approximate.
- **Seller-type cells:** quota targets cannot be benchmarked against official data, so a 50/50 seller-type quota is a design choice, not a population mirror. Options:
  - (a) Keep seller type as a quota for analytic comparison and say explicitly that it is not proportional.
  - (b) Use the Hub ~60/40 estimate as a soft reference, labelled as an industry estimate.
- **Registro Civil cannot reveal seller type.** Consignment sales ("orden de venta" / "consignación") are common in Chilean automotoras. In those, the transfer can be registered directly from the previous owner to the buyer, so a company sale can look person-to-person in the registry. A Corte Suprema case seen in search results describes exactly this ([pjud.cl](https://www.pjud.cl/prensa-y-comunicaciones/getRulingNew/78576), search snippet only, not fetched). Seller type must therefore be self-reported in the questionnaire, with clear definitions (particular / automotora-compraventa / concesionario de marca / plataforma such as Kavak).

## 2. Chilean studies and surveys of used-car buyers (demographics, channel, payment, inspection, fraud)

### Takeaway
No public, methodologically documented Chilean survey of used-car buyers was found. Not found from Autofact, Chileautos, Ipsos, GfK, Kantar or SERNAC. The usable material is: one Cadem online survey on car-purchase intention (2022, n = 2,318), Kavak's H1 2026 company report (no sample or method stated), and CAVEM registry-based sex and buyer-type splits. Information on inspection behaviour, fraud experience and willingness to pay for reports is a real data gap, which supports InfoCar's primary research.

### Cited Findings
- **Cadem online survey ("El Chile que viene"), n = 2,318 online interviews, national, published 14 Mar 2022:**
  - 71% of households own at least one car (63% in 2018, 53% in 2014).
  - Ownership by NSE: D/E 61%, C3 74%, C2 81%, C1 91%.
  - 30% planned to buy a car that year. Of these, 40% prefer new, 34% used, 26% either. Used preference is highest in the lowest NSE (40%).
  - Reasons for used: price 85%, direct deal with owner 35%.
  - Channel for used buyers: internet portals 55%, automotoras 42%.
  - Best-known portals: Chileautos 30%, Yapo 24%.
  - The fieldwork dates as printed ("lunes 7 a miércoles 9 de enero") are internally inconsistent with 2022.
  - — [La Tercera/Pulso, 14 Mar 2022](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/mas-del-70-de-los-hogares-en-chile-tiene-automovil-y-30-de-las-personas-preve-comprar-uno-este-ano/NMRXY4G7EJGBTFUNZKJ7EC76EM/)
- **Kavak "Reporte del Primer Semestre 2026" (Kavak's own clients; no sample or method published):**
  - Financed purchases of semi-new cars: 50% in H1 2026 vs 34% in 2025.
  - Financing by age: 18–23: 58% financed; 30–37: 50/50; 55+: 56% cash.
  - Market-wide, financing ("bancarización") is "apenas un 20%" in used cars vs >70% in new.
  - Top models: MG 3, Mazda 3, Suzuki Swift, MG ZS, KIA Morning.
  - — [Chócale, 31 Aug 2026](https://chocale.cl/2026/08/compra-de-autos-usados-en-chile-financiamiento-supera-al-pago-al-contado/)
- **Kavak 2025 report (unverified):** reportedly 58% men / 42% women among semi-new buyers. Seen only in a search summary; the article was not fetched.
- **CAVEM registry split, Oct 2024:** men 57%, women 30%, companies 14% of used transfers. — [24horas, 18 Nov 2024](https://www.24horas.cl/motores-24/dato-motor/compra-de-autos-usados-en-chile-quienes-adquieren-mas-estos-vehiculos)
- **SERNAC buyer guidance (Feb 2019):** SERNAC recommends:
  - an independent mechanic;
  - the Registro Civil "certificado de anotaciones vigentes" before buying;
  - a copy of the padrón after the transfer;
  - buying in "locales establecidos".
  - — [SERNAC, "Recomendaciones para comprar un auto usado", 21 Feb 2019](https://www.sernac.cl/portal/607/w3-article-1256.html)
- **CAVEM list of main risks (Aug 2026):** odometer tampering, hidden mechanical faults, informal contracts, scattered records. For private sales: fake payment receipts, reversible deposits, identity theft. No incidence figures were given. — [G5 Noticias, 31 Aug 2026](https://g5noticias.cl/2026/08/31/cavem-llama-a-verificar-el-historial-de-los-vehiculos-usados-antes-de-comprar/)

### Inferences
- **Payment criterion.** The draft's eligibility rule "paid with own/household money or personal credit" covers almost the whole market. Kavak puts market-wide financing at only about 20%. The variable should be recorded (cash / consumer credit / automotive credit / mixed) but not quota'd. Financing is concentrated among young buyers (Kavak).
- **Sex and age.** About two-thirds of person buyers are men (CAVEM Oct 2024). An unweighted convenience sample from car-enthusiast digital communities would likely over-represent men even more. Recording sex and post-stratifying or weighting to roughly 65/35 is defensible with this CAVEM source.
- **Cadem 2022 channel figure.** "55% portals vs 42% automotoras" refers to where people search, not the seller type. It must not be cited as a private vs dealer split.

### Gaps
- No Chileautos or Autofact consumer study with a published sample size was found on inspection behaviour, fraud experience or willingness to pay for vehicle-history or inspection reports.
- No SERNAC study of used-car complaints (counts or share by seller type) was retrieved in this pass.
- No public study from Criteria, GfK, Ipsos or Kantar on used-car buyers was found.

## 4. AIM socioeconomic classification (GSE AIM 2023): categories, distribution, classification questions

### Takeaway
The current standard is the **GSE AIM 2023** (published January 2024, built on CASEN 2022). It has seven groups: AB, C1a, C1b, C2, C3, D, E. Groups are assigned from three variables: household income per equivalent capita (with household size), and education and occupation of the main earner ("principal sostenedor del hogar", PSH). Classifying requires 4 questions (P1–P4) that can be asked anonymously online.

### Cited Findings
- **Method:**
  - Variables: "Ingreso per cápita equivalente", "Educación PSH", "Ocupación PSH".
  - Index: ISE-YEO = YTOTH/n^0.7 × EPSH × OPSH.
  - Parameters come from CASEN 2022, merged with CASEN 2017 for comunas. Results are expanded with 2022 projections based on Censo 2017.
  - Groups are cut at successive 0.05 intervals of the index, starting from the poverty line.
  - — [AIM, "Actualización y Manual de Aplicación GSE AIM 2023", Jan 2024](https://aimchile.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Actualizacion-y-Manual-GSE-AIM-2023_VF.pdf), pp. 4–11
- **Questions (pp. 16–20):**
  - Header: "Le vamos a solicitar dos informaciones sobre el principal sostenedor del hogar (PSH), es decir, sobre aquella persona que aporta los mayores ingresos al presupuesto del hogar".
  - **P1:** "¿Cuál es el nivel educacional alcanzado (último año aprobado) por el principal sostenedor del hogar?" — 10 options, from "Sin estudios formales" to "Postgrado".
  - **P2:** "¿Cuál de las siguientes ocupaciones corresponde al trabajo del principal sostenedor del hogar?" — if unemployed or retired, ask for the last paid occupation; if more than one job, use the highest-paid one.
  - **P3:** "Incluyéndose usted ¿cuántas personas viven en su hogar en la actualidad? (No considere servicio doméstico, aunque sea puertas adentro)".
  - **P4:** "...¿En cuál de estos tramos está el ingreso total mensual de su hogar?" — the 7 income brackets shown depend on the P3 answer.
  - Online instructions: show the 10 education options on screen; show occupation classifications at 1 and 2 digits.
  - — [AIM GSE 2023 manual](https://aimchile.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Actualizacion-y-Manual-GSE-AIM-2023_VF.pdf)
- **Income brackets, monthly household income in thousands of CLP (p. 21):**

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- **Notaría / Registro Civil intercepts.** These catch buyers at the moment of purchase, so incidence is very high. But they under-represent online transfers (about 25–30%), which are likely dealer-heavy given Autofact's automotora base. This biases the seller-type mix toward private sales, so quota control by seller type is essential. Notary access needs the notary's permission, and interviews must be short (5–8 min) and anonymous.
- **PRT intercepts.** The inspection date follows the vehicle's own calendar, not the purchase date, so only a fraction of people at a PRT bought their car in the last 12 months. Waiting time at PRT (queues) makes 5–10 min interviews feasible. The 41 RM facilities allow geographic spread across comunas, which helps NSE variety.
- **Coverage within the region.** Both intercept types cover only Santiago (RM) in the draft. Valparaíso and Biobío respondents would then come only from CAWI, which adds a mode-by-region confound. Note this as a limitation or add CAWI quotas by region.

## 7. Legal: legal warranty on used cars (Ley 19.496 / Ley 21.398) and Ley 21.719 on personal data

### Takeaway
The draft's claim is wrong. Ley 21.398 (published 24 Dec 2021) extended the legal warranty from 3 to 6 months from 24 Mar 2022, but under art. 14 of Ley 19.496 the warranty does not apply to used goods sold as used. SERNAC states the legal warranty is "aplicable solo a los productos nuevos". When buying from a company, the buyer has a right to truthful information plus any voluntary warranty the dealer offers, which becomes binding once agreed. A private one-off seller is not a "proveedor", so Ley 19.496 does not apply to that sale.

Ley 21.719 was published on 13 Dec 2024 and takes effect on 1 Dec 2026 unless a postponement is published first. The postponement bill (to 15 Oct 2027) passed the Senate on 28 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Ley 19.496 art. 14, inciso 1:** "Cuando con conocimiento del proveedor se expendan productos con alguna deficiencia, usados o refaccionados ... se deberán informar de manera expresa las circunstancias antes mencionadas al consumidor, antes de que éste decida la operación de compra."
- **Inciso 3:** "El cumplimiento de lo dispuesto en el inciso anterior eximirá al proveedor de las obligaciones derivadas del derecho de opción que se establece en los artículos 19 y 20, sin perjuicio de aquellas que hubiera contraído el proveedor en virtud de la garantía otorgada al producto."
- The site shows art. 14 with no modifications, consolidated by DFL-3 of 31 May 2021.
- — [Ley 19.496, art. 14 (iura.cl, mirror of the consolidated text)](https://iura.cl/19496/14.html). Official text: [BCN LeyChile, idNorma 61438](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=61438) (did not load in automated fetch).
- **SERNAC on used products:** "La Ley del Consumidor indica que la garantía legal es aplicable solo a los productos nuevos." If the used condition was disclosed, "usted no tendrá derecho a ejercer una garantía, salvo que la empresa voluntariamente le haya otorgado una". If it was not disclosed, the right to truthful information is breached and the buyer can complain. — [SERNAC, "¿Puedo exigir garantía legal por un producto usado?"](https://www.sernac.cl/portal/617/w3-article-57428.html) (no date shown)
- **SERNAC on used cars specifically:**
  - The cars "no tienen garantía legal", but buyers "sí tienen derecho a la información veraz y oportuna".
  - If the company does not disclose crashes, mechanical problems, unpaid fines, liens (prenda) or lawsuits, "no valen aquellas cláusulas" saying the buyer accepted the car as is.
  - A voluntary warranty, "Una vez que este beneficio es convenido con el consumidor, su cumplimiento pasa a ser obligatorio."
  - — [SERNAC Educación, 21 Feb 2019](https://www.sernac.cl/portal/607/w3-article-1256.html)
- **Who counts as a "proveedor" (Ley 19.496 art. 1 Nº2):** "las personas naturales o jurídicas, de carácter público o privado, que habitualmente desarrollen actividades de ... comercialización de bienes ... a consumidores, por las que se cobre precio o tarifa." — [Ley 19.496, art. 1 (iura.cl)](https://iura.cl/19496/1.html)
- **Ley 21.398 dates:** published in the Diario Oficial on 24 Dec 2021. The 3→6 month extension had a 3-month vacancy and applies to purchases from 24 Mar 2022. — [Diario Oficial, 24 Dec 2021 (PDF)](https://www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl/publicaciones/2021/12/24/43136/01/2061725.pdf); [SERNAC news](https://www.sernac.cl/portal/604/w3-article-64993.html); [El Mostrador, 24 Mar 2022](https://www.elmostrador.cl/datos-utiles/2022/03/24/que-deberan-cumplir-las-empresas-entra-en-vigencia-derecho-a-garantia-legal-de-seis-meses/)
- **Contrary view (law-firm blog, minority):**
  - Claims case law allows the legal warranty for a serious failure in a used car bought from a dealer.
  - Points to Civil Code hidden-defect actions.
  - Academic commentary (Ars Boni et Aequi) criticises art. 14 and cites a ruling limiting the warranty to new goods.
  - — [JO Abogados](https://joabogados.cl/garantia-legal-vehiculo/); [Ars Boni et Aequi (UBO)](https://arsboni.ubo.cl/index.php/arsboni/article/download/34/26)
- **Ley 21.719 publication and original date:** "La Ley N° 21.719 fue publicada en el Diario Oficial el 13 de diciembre de 2024". It takes effect on "el día primero del mes vigésimo cuarto posterior a su publicación", i.e. 1 Dec 2026. — [Garrigues, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.garrigues.com/es_ES/noticia/senado-chileno-aprueba-proyecto-ley-busca-postergar-15-octubre-2027-entrada-vigencia-nueva)
- **Postponement bill:**
  - Government bill entered the Senate on 1 Sep 2026, originally proposing 1 Dec 2027. — [Carey, 3 Sep 2026](https://www.carey.cl/gobierno-ingresa-proyecto-de-ley-que-posterga-en-un-ano-entrada-en-vigor-de-la-ley-sobre-proteccion-de-datos-personales)
  - The Senate approved Boletín N° 18.623-07 on 28 Oct 2026, which "fija como nueva fecha de entrada en vigencia de dicha ley el 15 de octubre de 2027", and sent it to the Cámara for its second step.
  - Until the bill is published as law, "se mantiene la fecha de entrada en vigencia prevista en la Ley N° 21.719."
  - — [Garrigues, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.garrigues.com/es_ES/noticia/senado-chileno-aprueba-proyecto-ley-busca-postergar-15-octubre-2027-entrada-vigencia-nueva)

### Inferences
