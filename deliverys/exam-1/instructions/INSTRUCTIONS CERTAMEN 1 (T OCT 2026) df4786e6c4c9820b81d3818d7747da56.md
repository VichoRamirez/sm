# INSTRUCTIONS CERTAMEN 1 (T OCT 2026)

## CERTUMEN 1 (t)

## DEFINING MARKET RESEARCH METHODOLOGY AND DATA COLLECTION TECHNIQUES

This assignment builds on previous stages. It must therefore incorporate the connections, adjustments, and recommendations provided earlier, keeping the overall project up to date. These improvements will be reviewed and form part of the assessment.

Before starting this stage, the business diagnosis and the general and specific research objectives must be completed and corrected, since methodological decisions must follow from and be consistent with them.

This stage focuses on defining the research methodology and data collection techniques, applying the criteria and concepts covered in class.

## The assignment must:

- Define the research methodology, specifying whether the study will be:
- Qualitative
- Quantitative
- Mixed methods

The decision must be justified based on the business diagnosis and the general and specific research objectives, explaining how the selected methodology addresses the identified information needs.

- If a mixed methods approach is selected, explain how the qualitative and quantitative components will be combined:
    - Sequential or phased: one methodology is conducted first, and its findings inform the next. Specify the order and explain why.
    - Parallel: both methodologies are conducted simultaneously or independently to address different objectives or information needs.

Justify this decision and explain how each methodology contributes to achieving the research objectives.

- Define the quantitative data collection technique(s), where applicable, selecting one or more of the following:
    - In-depth interviews / one-on-one interviews.
    - Focus groups.
    - Ethnography or observation, specifying non-participant or participant observation.

For each selected technique, explain what information it will provide and why it is superior to the research problem. It can be multiple qualitative techniques, justify the specific contribution of each.

- Define the quantitative data collection technique(s), where applicable, selecting one or more of the following:

Face-to-face (F2F) interviewing, specifying the mode of administration:

- At home / at workplace.
- In Situ, in a setting directly related to the phenomenon being studied.
- At high-traffic locations.
- CATI (Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing) / telephone surveys.
- CAWI (Computer-Assisted Web Interviewing) / online surveys.

A combination of two or more modes, when appropriate.

- For each selected technique and mode, justify its suitability for reaching the target population, the information it will generate, and its contribution to the research objectives.
- If more than one data collection technique is proposed, explain how they complement one another and the role each plays in the overall research design.

Important: Methodology and techniques must be selected based on the research problem and
objectives, not personal preferences, costs or inconvenience. The immediate availability, naming
a methodology or technique is not enough; every decision must be properly justified.

Note: At this stage, there is no need to describe the target population in detail or define
the survey size and composition. Those decisions will be addressed in the next stage,
based on the methodology and techniques selected in this workshop.
