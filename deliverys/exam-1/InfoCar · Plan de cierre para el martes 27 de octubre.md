**Objetivos propuestos para el slide 10** (mismo objetivo general, ajustes marcados en negrita):

- **SO1:** Identify the physical, **mechanical** and aesthetic attributes most valued when weighing a car's condition. → Detection catalogue
- **SO2:** Analyze how anticipating reconditioning costs affects the purchase offer **and the expected margin**. → Pricing rules
- **SO3:** Evaluate the distrust barriers that affect decisions under information asymmetry, **including brands with little repair history in Chile**. → Confidence scoring
- **SO4:** Describe the time bottlenecks of conventional inspection and repair costing. → B2B sales pitch
- **SO5:** Compare willingness to pay **and the preferred payment format (subscription or one-off report) between dealers and private buyers**. → Segment and price decision
