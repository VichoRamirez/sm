## Por qué el guion dice lo que dice

- **El PESTEL describe; el FODA clasifica.** Hasta la lámina 43 no se dice “opportunity” ni “threat”.
- **Dato sin fuente, dato de baja.** Cada cifra nueva se dice con su fuente (“says the INE”). La cita completa está en la lámina.
- **Cada fuerza dice de qué letra nace.** El chip amarillo (“From PESTEL: T · E”) se lee en voz alta.
- **Los límites se dicen antes de que los pregunten:** 31 % de contenido inventado, 52,3 días como proxy y umbrales propuestos por el equipo.
- **InfoCar no es “un chatbot”.** Decir “damage reports” o “damage recognition”. El chatbot es el sustituto.
- **Las metas no son objetivos de investigación.** Un SO valida una meta; la meta es lo que hay que lograr.

Good morning, professor. We are InfoCar: we help used-car dealers in Chile price a car knowing its repair cost.
Last time we showed the PESTEL. Today we close the loop: what changed since then, the industry around us, a SWOT, four strategic lines, and the goals that make them measurable.

## 35 · Qué cambió en el PESTEL · Dani · 0:55

First, the PESTEL is not frozen. Three facts changed since our last presentation.
Economic: economists now expect zero growth for 2026, says the Central Bank's survey, and unemployment rose to 9.8 percent, says the INE.
Political: after a truckers' strike, the government cut diesel by 142 pesos a litre and froze fuel prices until March.
Legal: the data law now starts on 15 October 2027. Law 21.855 made it official. What is still open is whether its agency will be ready.
Used-car transfers still grow, so the picture holds. The contrast is just sharper.

## 36 · La industria · Seba · 0:35

We define the industry as information on a used car's condition and price, before money changes hands: for a dealer, a buyer, and, as you suggested, an insurer. Five rules: industry first, built on the PESTEL, a level with reasons, high is not bad, and assumptions labelled. Where the PESTEL lacked industry data, we added it to E and T, with sources.

## 37 · Rivalidad: Medium · Seba · 0:25

The used-car market grows 3%, says The Clinic with CAVEM data. History, price and inspection are each covered by someone. Nobody joins them with the car's damage. Rivalry is medium: the contest is over who joins the layers.

## 38 · Nuevos entrantes: Medium–high · Vicho · 0:30

Inchcape is piloting repair quotes from photos for BMW owners in Chile and Peru. Corfo funded 145 startups this year, and image AI costs about a dollar and a half per thousand images. Entry is cheap for the technology and costly for local data: that is where our barrier must be.

## 39 · Sustitutos: High · Gabo · 0:35

The strongest force is a phone and a free chatbot: 78% of Chileans have used generative AI. But only 21 percent trust AI chatbots, says Ipsos, and even a model anchored to car damage invents content in 31% of reports. Used is not trusted, and free is not reliable. So we do not sell a chatbot: we sell damage recognition, with proof.

## 40 · Compradores: High · Bruno · 0:35

The average dealer buys about three cars a month and can go back to a spreadsheet at no cost. Insurers are a third buyer, with 2.23 million insured cars, but Mapfre already has its own photo app. We start with a monthly plan, and SO5 asks each segment how it prefers to pay. Insurers go to the next study.

## 41 · Proveedores: Medium · Checho · 0:30

AI vendors are many and cheap. The scarce input is workshop price data: 29 days just to get a car into a workshop, according to the CMF. That figure is for insured cars, so we use it as a proxy. Workshops are not in our sample: they are the next study.

## 42 · Cinco fuerzas · Checho · 0:15

No force is low, and two are high. As we heard in class, a high force is not a bad business: it shows what to fix before launch.

## 43–44 · Selección · Checho · 0:30

Now we move from describing to choosing. A finding passes to the SWOT if it touches the information gap at purchase, holds today, and we can act on it now. Of 38 findings, 24 pass, 6 stay on watch, like the used-EV import bill, and 8 only frame the market.

## 45–46 · FODA · Dani · 0:50

Every item shows where it comes from: a PESTEL letter or a force. Opportunities: a growing market in an economy that does not grow, no legal warranty on used cars, warranties that run out, insurers, and Chinese brands without a resale curve. Threats: the free chatbot, entrants within reach, early digital firms, data and AI rules, and buyers who can leave. Inside, our strength is joining damage, repair cost and price; our weaknesses are what a photo cannot see and the data we still lack.

## 47 · Cuatro líneas · Vicho · 0:30

Each line crosses inside and outside, and each name says what we build. One: verified damage reports, that show what they saw and what they cannot see. Two: a price and repair database, first with dealers' real offers, then with workshops. Three: a pre-inspection for insurers. Four: a monthly plan for dealers.

## 48 · Metas · Gabo · 0:50

And each line has a goal. One hundred mechanic-checked reports, with at most 10% invented content, against a 31% baseline. Real prices from 60 dealers, and at least three workshops sharing theirs. Three or four insurer interviews and one pilot deal. And a marketing goal: 10 of the 60 dealers we study sign the monthly plan. Research objectives tell us what to understand; these goals tell us what to achieve. Two are checked by this study, two by the next one.

## 49 · Cierre · Bruno · 0:20

## Preguntas probables

- **“¿Por qué 0 % si el Banco Central dijo 0,25–0,75 %?”** Ese rango es del IPoM de septiembre. La encuesta de expectativas de noviembre del mismo Banco Central dice 0 %, después de que el PIB cayera 1,1 % en el tercer trimestre. El IPoM de diciembre puede actualizarlo.
- **“Si pocos confían en los chatbots, ¿por qué sustitutos sigue alto?”** Porque el uso es masivo y gratis: el 78 % ya lo usó. La baja confianza no reduce la fuerza; es nuestra entrada, y por eso vendemos reconocimiento de daños con prueba.
- **“¿La meta de suscripción es de marketing?”** Sí: es una meta de adquisición. Mide cuántos dealers del estudio pasan a pagar (10 de 60).
- **“¿De dónde salen los umbrales?”** Son propuestas del equipo, cada una con su argumento. El SO que valida cada meta puede subirla o bajarla antes de comprometerla.
- **“¿La ley de datos ya rige?”** No. La Ley 21.855, publicada el 23 de noviembre de 2026, la movió al 15 de octubre de 2027.
